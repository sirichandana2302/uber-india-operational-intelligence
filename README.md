# Uber India Operational Data Intelligence

## Project Overview
This project develops an end-to-end Business Analytics solution to analyze demand-supply imbalance in Uber India's ride-sharing operations across 10 Indian cities.

The project uses:
- MySQL
- SQL
- Microsoft Power BI
- Excel

The objective is to identify operational inefficiencies, surge pricing patterns, revenue concentration, and driver performance trends.

## Business Problem
Uber India faces:
- High trip cancellations
- Demand-supply imbalance
- Surge pricing inefficiencies
- Driver performance inconsistency

This project analyzes operational data to provide actionable business insights.

## Dataset Information
- 300 trip records
- 50 driver profiles
- 10 Indian cities
- Time Period: Jan 2024 – Mar 2024

Cities Covered:
- Ahmedabad

- Chennai
- Delhi

- Jaipur

## Key Insights
### 1. Demand-Supply Gap
- Only 72.3% of trips were successfully completed
- 27.7% of demand went unserved

### 2. Top Revenue City
- Ahmedabad generated the highest revenue

### 3. Peak Demand Window
- Late Night (12 AM – 5 AM) recorded highest trip volume

### 4. Airport Revenue Impact
- Airport pickups generated 2.75x higher revenue than regular trips

### 5. Driver Quality
- Only 28.9% of drivers qualified as Star Drivers
